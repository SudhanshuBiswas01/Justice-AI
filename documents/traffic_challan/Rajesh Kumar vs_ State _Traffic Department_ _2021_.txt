In the High Court of Delhi at New Delhi.
Writ Petition (Civil) No. 4920/2021.
Decided on: November 3, 2021.

Rajesh Kumar (Petitioner) vs. State (Traffic Department) & Ors (Respondents).

JUDGMENT:
The petitioner has filed this writ petition challenging a traffic e-challan of Rs. 1,000 issued under Section 194D of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, for allegedly riding a two-wheeler without a protective headgear (helmet).

The petitioner submits that on the date of the alleged offense, October 10, 2021, he was indeed wearing a helmet. The e-challan issued by the automated camera system contained a photograph of the petitioner. Upon inspection of the photograph, it is clear that the petitioner was wearing a standard helmet, but the camera's image recognition algorithm misidentified the strap as absent, triggering the automated challan.

The Traffic Department argues that the e-challan was generated by an automated, state-of-the-art Speed and Violation Detection System and that manual checks are performed before issuance.

LEGAL PRINCIPLES:
Section 129 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, mandates every person driving or riding a motorcycle to wear protective headgear conforming to standards. Section 194D prescribes penalties for non-compliance.

However, technology-driven enforcement must be free of material error. Automated challans based purely on machine-learning algorithms cannot displace the rule of fair evidence. If the visual evidence attached to the challan clearly refutes the claim of violation, the challan must be declared null and void.

DECISION:
The High Court, after visual verification of the photographic evidence, finds that the petitioner was indeed wearing a helmet. The automated system erred in its classification. The traffic e-challan No. DL-2049102948 is hereby quashed.
1. The Traffic Department is directed to delete the challan record immediately.
2. The respondents are ordered to implement a manual verification layer for all e-challans generated by automated cameras before they are officially dispatched to vehicle owners.
